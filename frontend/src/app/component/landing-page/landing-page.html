Frequently Asked Questions

Can I track the live location of my bus?

Yes! Look for buses marked with the Live Tracking badge on the search results page. Once booked, you'll receive a live GPS tracking link via SMS and email, and you can also track it directly inside your account.

What is TedBus Reschedulable ticket feature?

Buses with the Reschedulable tag allow you to alter your travel date or time up to 8 hours before scheduled departure without incurring heavy cancellation fees.

What payment methods are supported on TedBus?

We support all major payment modes including instant UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, QR scan), Credit & Debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), Net Banking for over 40+ banks, and digital wallets.

Can I charter an entire bus for private groups or events?

Yes, use our Custom Busses option directly from the search bar or header to hire sanitized, air-conditioned buses for weddings, corporate tours, and pilgrim groups with custom pickup points.

How do I get discounts on my bus bookings?

First-time users can use code FIRST or FIRST50 for instant discounts. For festival and seasonal savings, check our Trending Offers strip or use code TEDFEST on checkout.
